# FlashForge Quick Start Guide 🎴

**Create professional flashcards in 5 minutes**

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Open FlashForge in your browser
2. Click **"New Set"**
3. Name your set (e.g., "Spanish Verbs Unit 1")
4. Click **"Add Card"** to start adding flashcards

---

## ✏️ Adding Cards

**Front Text**: The word, question, or prompt  
**Back Text**: The translation, answer, or definition  
**Image**: Optional picture (click image icon to upload)

**Pro tip**: For vocabulary, put the target language word on the front and the English translation on the back.

---

## 🖨️ Printing Your Flashcards

### Step 1: Preview
Click the **"Preview"** tab to see how your cards will look when printed.

### Step 2: Choose Layout
Select how many cards per page:
- **2 per page** = large cards (half-page size)
- **4 per page** = quarter-page cards (popular size)
- **8 per page** = playing card size
- **12 per page** = small study cards

### Step 3: Print to PDF
1. Click the **"Print"** button
2. In the print dialog, select **"Save as PDF"**
3. Set margins to **"None"** or **"Minimum"**
4. Make sure scale is **100%**
5. Save your PDF

### Step 4: Print & Cut
1. Open the PDF and print it
2. Cut along the dotted lines
3. For durability, use cardstock or laminate

---

## 🔄 Double-Sided Cards (Advanced)

For cards you can flip over:

1. Print the **front pages** first
2. Take the printed stack and **flip it** (test with one sheet first!)
3. Re-insert into printer
4. Print the **back pages**
5. Cut apart - fronts and backs should align

**Tip**: Most printers flip on the long edge (portrait mode).

---

## 💡 Quick Tips

✅ **Images**: Use square images (800x800px) for best results  
✅ **Text**: Keep it short - 1-3 words works best  
✅ **Paper**: Use cardstock (110 lb) for sturdier cards  
✅ **Laminating**: Cut cards slightly large, laminate, then trim to size  
✅ **Storage**: Save ~5-10 sets comfortably in your browser  
✅ **Backup**: Your data is saved automatically in your browser

## ⚠️ Troubleshooting

**Cards are cut off?**  
→ Set print margins to "None" or "Minimum"

**Text too small?**  
→ Use fewer cards per page (try 4 instead of 12)

**Images blurry?**  
→ Use larger source images (800px+) and always "Save as PDF"

**Lost my cards?**  
→ Data is saved in your browser - use the same browser and URL

**Want to back up?**  
→ Export feature coming soon! For now, keep your PDFs

## 🎯 Workflow for a Full Unit

1. Create a new set for each unit/chapter
2. Add 15-30 cards with key vocabulary
3. Print 4 per page for whole class
4. Print 8-12 per page for individual student sets
5. Laminate one class set for durability
6. Print cardstock copies for students to take home
